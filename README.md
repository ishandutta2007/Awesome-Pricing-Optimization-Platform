Last updated: September 2026

This repository tracks notable SaaS platforms and open-source projects for Pricing Optimization. These tools help organizations determine optimal prices by analyzing demand, customer behavior, price elasticity, competitor pricing, inventory, costs, promotions, market conditions, and business constraints.

Examples include PROS, Zilliant, Pricefx, Vendavo, Competera, Prisync, Omnia Retail, BlackCurve, Revionics, and IntelligenceNode (the category leaders).

Open-source emphasis: The open-source pricing optimization ecosystem is more fragmented than commercial enterprise pricing software, but it contains a growing collection of dynamic-pricing engines, demand forecasting systems, causal inference frameworks, reinforcement-learning projects, mathematical optimization libraries, and self-hosted analytics components. These projects are particularly useful for organizations building transparent and customizable pricing systems without vendor lock-in.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

Open-Source GitHub Projects

NeuroPrice / Dynamic Pricing Algorithms
Open-source dynamic pricing engine combining reinforcement learning and causal inference for price-demand modeling and intelligent pricing strategies.

Pricing Engine
End-to-end open-source dynamic pricing and revenue optimization engine combining an XGBoost demand model with profit-maximizing optimization and a FastAPI service layer.

Retail Price Optimization
Machine-learning-based retail pricing optimization project that analyzes competitor pricing, demand, customer behavior, and market trends to recommend revenue-oriented prices.

Smart Dynamic Pricing
AI-powered dynamic pricing system using deep reinforcement learning, customer behavior simulation, Flask APIs, and a React dashboard.

Retail Price Optimization using Deep Reinforcement Learning
Open-source research implementation applying deep reinforcement learning to retail pricing decisions involving demand, competition, inventory, and profit margins.

E-Commerce Dynamic Pricing Optimization
Causal machine-learning pricing pipeline combining Double Machine Learning, demand forecasting, elasticity estimation, optimization, Docker deployment, and API serving.

PricePilot Dynamic Pricing System
AI-driven dynamic pricing project combining XGBoost demand forecasting, time-series forecasting, SHAP explainability, PPO reinforcement learning, and an interactive Streamlit interface.

E-Commerce Demand Forecasting and Dynamic Pricing
Hybrid AI pricing architecture combining demand forecasting with deterministic margin and business-rule constraints for e-commerce price optimization.

Pricing and Promotion Decision Engine
Open-source retail pricing and promotion engine supporting demand estimation, price elasticity analysis, counterfactual simulation, pricing constraints, and experiment design.

Price Optimization GitHub Topic
Community collection of public repositories covering price optimization, dynamic pricing, demand forecasting, revenue optimization, and pricing strategy experimentation.

OR-Tools
Google's open-source mathematical optimization suite supporting linear programming, mixed-integer optimization, constraint programming, routing, and other optimization problems that can be used to build pricing engines.

Pyomo
Open-source Python-based optimization modeling framework suitable for formulating constrained price optimization, revenue maximization, and pricing portfolio problems.

CVXPY
Open-source convex optimization framework useful for implementing pricing optimization models involving margins, demand constraints, budgets, and business rules.

SciPy Optimize
General-purpose open-source scientific computing library containing optimization algorithms useful for numerical price optimization and parameter estimation.

Optuna
Open-source hyperparameter optimization framework useful for tuning demand forecasting and pricing models.

Stable-Baselines3
Popular open-source reinforcement-learning library that can be used to build and train dynamic pricing agents using PPO, DQN, A2C, and other algorithms.

Ray RLlib
Scalable open-source reinforcement-learning framework suitable for distributed experimentation and large-scale dynamic pricing simulations.

Gymnasium
Open-source reinforcement-learning environment framework useful for creating custom pricing environments with inventory, demand, competition, and customer-response simulations.

EconML
Microsoft's open-source causal machine-learning library that can help estimate treatment effects and price elasticity while accounting for confounding variables.

DoWhy
Open-source causal inference framework useful for evaluating the causal impact of pricing and promotions rather than relying solely on observational correlations.

CausalML
Open-source causal machine-learning toolkit that supports uplift modeling and heterogeneous treatment-effect estimation for pricing and promotion analysis.

DoubleML
Open-source framework for Double Machine Learning, useful for estimating causal price effects and reducing bias in price elasticity models.

XGBoost
Widely used open-source gradient-boosting framework suitable for demand forecasting and nonlinear price-response modeling.

LightGBM
High-performance open-source gradient-boosting framework frequently used for retail demand forecasting and pricing feature modeling.

CatBoost
Open-source gradient-boosting framework particularly useful when pricing datasets contain many categorical features such as products, regions, customer segments, and channels.

Prophet
Open-source time-series forecasting framework useful for modeling seasonality and demand trends that influence pricing decisions.

StatsForecast
Open-source time-series forecasting library supporting scalable statistical forecasting models for demand and revenue forecasting pipelines.

MLForecast
Open-source machine-learning forecasting framework that can support SKU-level demand forecasting as part of a pricing optimization system.

Darts
Open-source Python forecasting library supporting classical statistical models and deep-learning forecasting approaches.

scikit-learn
Foundational open-source machine-learning library useful for demand models, price elasticity estimation, segmentation, experimentation, and pricing pipelines.

MLflow
Open-source MLOps platform useful for tracking, versioning, comparing, and deploying demand forecasting and pricing optimization models.

Feast
Open-source feature store that can help production pricing systems manage historical and real-time features such as competitor prices, inventory, demand, and customer behavior.

Apache Superset
Open-source analytics and business-intelligence platform suitable for building pricing dashboards and monitoring pricing performance.

Metabase
Open-source business-intelligence platform that can be used to create self-hosted pricing, revenue, margin, and competitor-price dashboards.

Grafana
Open-source observability and visualization platform useful for real-time monitoring of price changes, revenue, margins, demand, and optimization outcomes.

Additional Strong Open-Source Options

Mathematical optimization frameworks: OR-Tools, Pyomo, CVXPY, SciPy Optimize, PuLP, and other linear, nonlinear, and mixed-integer optimization libraries.

Demand forecasting: Prophet, StatsForecast, MLForecast, Darts, GluonTS, NeuralForecast, sktime, and custom time-series pipelines.

Machine-learning pricing models: XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, scikit-learn, PyTorch, TensorFlow, and probabilistic demand models.

Reinforcement learning for dynamic pricing: Stable-Baselines3, Ray RLlib, Gymnasium, CleanRL, and custom simulation environments.

Causal price elasticity and experimentation: EconML, DoWhy, CausalML, DoubleML, and other causal inference frameworks.

Operations research and revenue optimization: PuLP, HiGHS, COIN-OR projects, OR-Tools, and solver integrations for constrained optimization.

Competitor and market intelligence pipelines: Scrapy, Playwright, Beautiful Soup, Apache Airflow, Prefect, Dagster, and custom web-data collection systems.

Analytics and dashboards: Apache Superset, Metabase, Grafana, Redash forks, Streamlit, and Plotly Dash.

Data infrastructure: PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, DuckDB, Apache Kafka, Apache Spark, and dbt for building scalable pricing data platforms.

Experimentation frameworks: GrowthBook, PlanOut-inspired experimentation approaches, statistical testing libraries, and custom A/B testing pipelines.

Many community projects for dynamic pricing, airline and hotel revenue management, e-commerce pricing, retail demand forecasting, price elasticity modeling, promotion optimization, and reinforcement-learning-based revenue management.

Frameworks for building custom systems: Combine PostgreSQL or ClickHouse for pricing data, Feast for feature management, XGBoost or LightGBM for demand forecasting, EconML or DoubleML for price elasticity estimation, OR-Tools or Pyomo for constrained optimization, and Stable-Baselines3 for dynamic pricing experimentation. Use MLflow for model management and Superset, Metabase, Grafana, or Streamlit for pricing analytics and operational dashboards.

For a production-grade self-hosted architecture, a practical workflow is:

Data Collection → Demand Forecasting → Elasticity Estimation → Constraint Modeling → Price Optimization → Experimentation → Monitoring → Continuous Model Retraining

Disclaimer

This is a community-curated list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

Pricing optimization recommendations should be reviewed for commercial, contractual, regulatory, competition-law, fairness, and consumer-protection implications.

Open-source machine-learning and optimization libraries are often building blocks rather than complete enterprise pricing platforms.

Dynamic pricing systems require reliable demand data, careful experimentation, monitoring for feedback loops, and appropriate business guardrails.

Self-hosted open-source solutions require engineering effort for data pipelines, integrations, security, scalability, model governance, and operational reliability.
